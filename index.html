About the data
River geometry is requested from the HydroRIVERS ArcGIS service configured in this application. The map draws all returned reaches within the African bounding area. The upstream-area selector is an optional display filter; it is not applied to the initial network download. Very small or ephemeral streams may not be represented in HydroRIVERS.

Reading the map
Each coloured line represents a river reach. The colour indicates its classified drainage destination. White moving dashes highlight selected major river reaches. The animation follows the coordinate direction of the returned geometry and is intended as a visual flow effect, not a verified measurement of water velocity or downstream direction.
